# Rules

## Components
- 104 number cards (8 sets, each 1–13)
- 5 joker cards ("X")
- 1 Linko card (tracks active player)

## Object of the Game
Lay down as many cards as possible. Each card played to your stack = **+1 point**. Each card remaining in hand at game end = **-1 point**. Snatch cards other players have already stacked to score them yourself — or force opponents to pick them back up.

## Setup
1. Place the Linko card in front of the youngest player.
2. Shuffle all remaining cards; deal **13 cards** to each player (kept hidden).
3. Form a face-down draw pile in the center. Place a discard pile space to its left.
4. Flip the top 6 cards face-up to the right of the draw pile to form the **card row**.

## Gameplay
Play clockwise starting with the youngest player. On your turn:

### 1. Play cards
Play **one card or multiple cards of the same number** face-up in front of you (your stack).
- New cards are placed on top of your existing stack; they do not need to match the number or count already in your stack.
- A joker played together with number cards takes on the same value as those number cards.
- A joker played alone has the highest value on the table (greater than 13).

### 2. Compare & Snatch (Abluxxen)
After playing, compare your played card(s) with the **top cards** of each other player's stack, moving clockwise, once.

**Snatching is triggered when BOTH conditions are met:**
- Your card(s) match the **number of cards** at the top of another player's stack, **AND**
- Your card(s) have a **higher card number**.

Snatching is **mandatory** — if both conditions are met you must snatch. A player may not be robbed more than once per turn.

#### The Dilemma: what happens to snatched cards?
**The active player decides first** whether they want the snatched cards:

- **YES — active player takes them:**
  The robbed player must immediately replenish their hand with the same number of cards stolen (drawn from the card row or draw pile, their choice, one card at a time).

- **NO — active player doesn't want them:**
  The robbed player then chooses:
  - **Pick up** the snatched cards back into their hand, **OR**
  - **Discard** them to the discard pile and draw the same number of new cards.

  The snatched cards **cannot remain in the stack**.

After all replenishment drawing is done, the card row is refilled to 6 cards (if possible).

If the active player snatches from multiple players, resolve each in clockwise order.

## Joker Rules
- Played with number cards → takes the same value as those number cards.
- Played alone → counts as higher than 13 (beats any number card).
- Example: two jokers snatch two 13s.

## End of Game
The game ends immediately when **either**:
- A player has **no cards left** in hand (no more snatching occurs after the last card is played), **OR**
- The **card row and draw pile are both exhausted** (can end mid-turn).

## Scoring
- Each card in your stack: **+1 point**
- Each card remaining in your hand: **-1 point**
- The number on the card does not matter for scoring.
- Highest total wins. Ties: all tied players win.

## Multi-Game Sessions
Write down points after each game. Rotate the start player so everyone starts once per session. Sum all points at the end; highest total wins.

## Don't Forget
1. Snatching is **not voluntary** — mandatory when conditions are met.
2. Single cards can be snatched (and can snatch others).
3. Snatching can happen as early as the first round (from the second player onward).
4. Each player plays cards **once per turn**.
5. Only the **active player** can snatch.
6. Only the **uppermost** cards in a stack can be snatched.
7. Snatched cards go to the active player's hand, the robbed player's hand, or the discard pile — **never stay in anyone's stack**.

## Tips
- **Decoy:** Play a low, easily-snatched card to draw a better card from the card row.
- **Safety in numbers:** Playing many low cards at once (e.g. six 2s) makes them hard to snatch.
- **Watch hand sizes:** The game can end faster than expected. Avoid picking up snatched cards when opponents are nearly out.
